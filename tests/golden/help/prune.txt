                                                                                                                        
 Usage: root prune [OPTIONS] SEARCH CANDIDATE_ID                                                                        
                                                                                                                        
 Prune a candidate and its whole subtree.                                                                               
                                                                                                                        
 The search stops building on this lineage and it is excluded from                                                      
 selection. Statuses and scores stay visible in status/tree output.                                                     
                                                                                                                        
╭─ Arguments ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
│ *    search            TEXT  [required]                                                                              │
│ *    candidate_id      TEXT  [required]                                                                              │
╰──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╯
╭─ Options ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
│ --reason          TEXT  Why this branch is being cut (recorded in the journal)                                       │
│ --help    -h            Show this message and exit.                                                                  │
