                                                                                                                        
 Usage: root top [OPTIONS]                                                                                              
                                                                                                                        
 The control pane for every hillclimb process on this machine.                                                          
                                                                                                                        
 `hillclimb ps`, live and with controls: the machine at a glance                                                        
 (searches, coding agents against the machine's slot cap, cpu, memory)                                                  
 above one table where each search heads its own process tree — coding                                                  
 agents, their tool shells and MCP servers, verifiers and the solution                                                  
 they score. Works from any folder. Keys act on the highlighted row:                                                    
 s=stop its search (resumable), g=stop gracefully, k=kill that process                                                  
 and what it started (on a search row: the whole search), o=change the                                                  
 order of the searches, r=reverse it, q=quit. Search progress is                                                        
 `hillclimb watch`.
